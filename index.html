Questions your data can already answer

Most businesses already have the numbers, spread across the POS, bookkeeping and spreadsheets. We turn them into answers you can act on each week.

  • “Will I have enough cash eight weeks from now?”

    Cash flow forecast

    A 13-week projection built from your bank, sales and bills, refreshed every week, with an alert before a shortfall shows up.

  • “Which customers stopped coming back, and who is next?”

    Customer insights

    Customer groups by value and buying habits, plus a list of customers at risk of leaving so your team can reach out in time.

  • “How much should I buy and staff for next month?”

    Demand forecast

    Expected sales by product, location and day of the week, including holidays and season, so you order and schedule with less waste.

  • “Where is my margin leaking?”

    Profit and KPI dashboard

    One page with the few numbers that matter for your business, updated automatically, with a note when something moves out of the normal range.

  • “Can AI take care of leads and paperwork for us?”

    AI assistants and automation

    Assistants that answer common customer questions, read invoices and receipts into your spreadsheet, and draft replies to reviews, always with a person in control.

  • “How does my team start using AI the right way?”

    Hands-on AI training

    Practical workshops for owners and staff, using your own processes as examples, in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Common questions

My data is messy and lives in spreadsheets. Is that a problem?

No. That is where most small businesses start. Organizing and cleaning the data is part of the first step, and we tell you which gaps matter and which do not.

How much does it cost?

Plans are monthly and depend on how many data sources and models you need. After the free data check you receive a fixed monthly quote in writing, before any work starts.

Is my data safe?

We request read-only access whenever the tool allows it, store data only for your project, never sell or share it, and delete it at the end of the engagement. We are glad to sign an NDA.

Which kinds of businesses do you work with?

Restaurants and cafes, retail and e-commerce, clinics, construction and home services, real estate and professional services. If you sell to customers and keep records, there is usually something useful to find.

Do you only work in South Florida?

We focus on Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach so we can meet in person, but all the work can be done remotely anywhere in the U.S.
