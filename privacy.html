Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the details you type: your name, email, business name, phone number if you add it, industry, team size and your message. This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to prepare the free data check you requested. We do not sell, rent or share them for marketing.

Services that help us

The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process the minimum technical data needed to run their service.

Client data in projects

When you hire us, the data you share for a project is used only for that project, accessed read-only whenever possible, never sold, and deleted at the end of the engagement, unless a written agreement says otherwise.

How long we keep it

Contact messages are kept for up to 24 months, or less if you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to contact@d4goal.com.
